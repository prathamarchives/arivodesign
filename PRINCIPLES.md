# PRINCIPLES — arivodesign

Design values that interpret the laws and the contract. Principles bind judgment where laws and rules cannot reach (the gray zones of taste, hierarchy, and trade-off). They are grounds, not procedure. Append-only; supersede by reference.

- **P-01 · The book is the interface.** The highest praise Arivo can earn is "I forgot I was in an app." Chrome, controls, and system surfaces exist to disappear during reading. [L-01, X-01]
- **P-02 · Raw over tidy where thinking happens.** Notes and journals are rough work — pen-on-paper honesty, diagrams allowed, mess allowed. Tidiness is for the library and the reading surface, not the mind. [X-04; owner vision words]
- **P-03 · Capture is sacred; structure is provisional.** Instant capture first, refinement optional and later. Nothing in the design may put a required field between the owner and a captured thought. [X-02, X-05; Arivoo P-10]
- **P-04 · Nothing compounds silently, nothing disappears loudly.** Growth (concepts, digest, library) should be visible; durability (highlights, notes, position) should be quiet and certain. [X-03, X-06]
- **P-05 · Desktop is the homeland.** Design for the large screen, the keyboard, the long session. Adaptation down is a later, honest translation — not a constraint that dilutes the core. [L-05, D-001]
- **P-06 · Contracts render; they never mutate.** The design layer renders Arivoo's objects and semantics with their names and behavior intact. Design freedom lives in form, never in contract meaning. [Part 1]
- **P-07 · Free text is the taxonomy.** Humans classify with words at capture speed; systems infer structure later if ever. No imposed trees, dropdowns, or mandatory categories. [X-08; Arivoo D-030]
- **P-08 · The garden, not the blueprint.** The repo grows loop by loop, failure by failure, layer by layer — pruned by verification, never pre-built into bureaucracy. Agents are gardeners; the owner is the climate. [INT-002/003; Arivoo ENGINEERING]
- **P-09 · Prose last.** Working artifacts over explanations about artifacts. A rule that cannot be operated is a wish; a check that cannot run is prose. [L-13; ENGINEERING constraint ladder]
- **P-10 · Start small, grow on failure.** Governance weight stays proportional to observed failure, not imagined failure — the owner's "right way this time". [L-18]
- **P-11 · Any agent can garden.** Everything an agent needs lives in the repo: read order, contract, loops, verify command. No agent-specific knowledge, no chat-only memory. [L-11, L-12; Arivoo D-028 pattern]
- **P-12 · The vision is painted, then tested, then trusted.** HTML/CSS artifacts pass through replicate → refine → iterate → change → test before anything is finalized — taste is verified against the contract, not asserted. [INT-002; L-16]
