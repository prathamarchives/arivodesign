CHAPTER ONE / THE PSYCHOLOGY OF EVERYDAY THINGS

Discoverability and the language of things

We encounter objects with an intention already in mind. Before we act, we look for a clue: where to hold, which way to turn, what might happen next.

Affordances define what actions are possible. Signifiers specify how people discover those possibilities.

p. 14 · ¶ 2

1 deep note

The distinction matters. An action can be physically possible and still remain invisible to the person trying to perform it. A door may allow a push, while its handle seems to ask for a pull.

Look at the world through this lens and small moments of hesitation become revealing. The question is not only what an object permits, but what it helps us understand.
